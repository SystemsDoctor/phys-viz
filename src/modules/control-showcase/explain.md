## What am I looking at?

A deliberately un-physical sandbox exercising every control kind the
shell supports: a draggable point **p**, an angle $\theta$, a
log-scaled stiffness $k$, a small expression $f(x)$, a display mode,
and a highlight toggle.

## What should I notice?

Toggle "Reference grid" and "Angle trace" to see layer visibility work
without any module code. Reveal "Predicted magnitude" to see predict
mode's reveal-gating in action.

## The equation

$$ |\vec{p}| = \sqrt{p_x^2 + p_y^2 + p_z^2} $$
